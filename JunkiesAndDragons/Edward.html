Edward

Baldragon the Wicked, Scourge of Dundee, Tyrant of Dundee Law, foe of Edward.

Overview

"You took them from me! My home, my parents, my sister-in-law and my brother. Mother, father, Fincana and Martin, friends and family. Curse you! Curse you, you wretched thing! I will kill you today you flying snake, I shall tear your soul apart! I am Edward, and mine is the name you shall forever fear!" - Edward

Edward was born in the town of Dundee in the summer of 1259. His family were fishers and trawled along the river Tay, leaving his upbringing mainly in the hands of his brother Martin. In the year 1265 Baldragon arrived in Dundee amid fire and flames. Martin managed to keep Edward safe and their parents were out fishing at the time of the attack, although their house was lost in the attack. 3 years later in 1268, another attack killed the boys parents, leaving Martin and Edward struggling to continue the family business until the sacrifices starts.

In 1279, a few days before Edwards 20th birthday, his sister-in-law Fincana was chosen to be the nineth sacrifice to Baldragon. Martin was enraged by the villages willingness to sacrifice his fiance and dragged Edward in a mad quest to try and kill Baldragon. The quest failed and resulted in Martins death in the flames of Baldragon. Edward survived only by running back to Dundee, but when he arrived he found ruins and death. Baldragon had beaten him there and made his fury known, burning a vast swathe of farmland and houses. If so many had not died in the attack it is doubtful that they would have survived the starvation and winter. Fearful and alone, Edward took his damaged boat and sailed to the city of Edinburgh to try and get help.

Upon arriving in Edinburgh Edward encountered Organic Jim, a wise master of the magical arts. Jim took Edward on as an apprentice after hearing his tale, teaching Edward how to use his strength, speed, and magical abilities. Tim, Jim's other apprentice, became a rival to Edward, and it would take five years of training before Edward could best him. Upon Edwards victory, Jim declared Edward ready for his final lesson, and revelaed the Forbidden Arts to him. Edward then left to return to Dundee and face Baldragon once and for all.

In the year 1285, 20 years after Baldragon first attacked Dundee, Edward returned to face down Baldragon once and for all. Baldragon mocked Edward, insulting his training, his master, and his dead brother. Cackling and mocking, Baldragon unwittingly caused his own demise by angering Edward into using the Forbidden Arts. Sacrificing his own mind and sanity for more power, Edward tore Baldragon from the sky and smote the drake upon the ruins of houses he had once burned. Unable to contend with an enraged and empowered Edward, Baldragon found himself overwhlemed, and in turn sacrificed his size for more power. This desperate attempt was not enough to do more than cling to life as his body succumbed to Edwards magics, resulting in Edwards draining Baldragon of his power and lifespan until Baldragon was a frozen copper husk curled in the center of Dundee town. Edward, his mind succumbing to the insanity of the Forbidden Arts, vowed to stand ready for when Baldragon would return, even if it would take a thousand years.

Edward remains in Dundee to this day, his stolen lifespan allowing him to remain Dundee's maddened protector.

Edward was born in the summer of 1259 in the town of Dundee, into a family of fishermen who made their living trawling the waters of the River Tay. His early childhood was spent around the docks and shoreline, although much of his upbringing was entrusted to his older brother, Martin. With their parents frequently away fishing, Martin took on much of the responsibility of looking after Edward, becoming something of a second parent to his younger sibling. The two brothers grew particularly close, with Edward looking up to Martin as both a protector and a role model.

Edward's childhood was relatively uneventful until the year 1265, when Baldragon descended upon Dundee in a storm of fire and destruction. Edward was only six years old at the time, and would likely have perished in the attack had Martin not managed to keep him safe. Their parents were fortunate enough to be out fishing on the River Tay when the dragon struck, although the family home was lost in the flames. The attack left a lasting impression on Edward, who grew up knowing that the dragon had taken almost everything his family had worked for.

The death of his parents

Three years after Baldragon's first attack, tragedy struck the family once again. In 1268, Baldragon claimed the lives of Edward's parents, leaving the two brothers entirely alone. With no other family to turn to, Martin and Edward were forced to continue their parents' fishing business themselves. Martin took on most of the work, while Edward gradually learned the trade and helped wherever he could. Although times were difficult, the brothers managed to keep the business alive, relying on one another to survive.

As the years passed, the people of Dundee continued to live under Baldragon's rule, sending a yearly sacrifice to the dragon in exchange for their continued safety. Edward and Martin grew up in the shadow of this arrangement, knowing that their town's survival depended upon surrendering one of its own people every year. While Edward had become accustomed to the terrible bargain, Martin had never truly accepted it. His hatred of Baldragon only grew stronger as the years passed.

The death of Martin

In 1279, only a few days before Edward's twentieth birthday, tragedy struck once again. Fincana, Martin's fiancee and future sister-in-law to Edward, was selected as the ninth sacrifice to Baldragon. Martin was enraged by the decision, refusing to accept that the woman he loved should be offered to the dragon simply to preserve the cowardly peace of Dundee. Having already lost their parents and their childhood home to Baldragon, Martin could no longer tolerate the suffering inflicted upon his family.

Determined to put an end to the dragon's reign, Martin dragged Edward into a desperate attempt to slay Baldragon before Fincana could be taken. The brothers had little chance of success, lacking the strength and knowledge required to challenge a creature of Baldragon's size and power. Nevertheless, Martin pressed on, driven by anger and grief rather than any realistic expectation of victory.

Their attempt ended in disaster. Baldragon was amused by the brothers' challenge, easily dismissing their efforts and turning his fury upon them. Martin was killed in the dragon's flames, leaving Edward to witness the death of the last remaining member of his immediate family. Edward himself survived only by fleeing back towards Dundee, abandoning the fight in order to escape the dragon's wrath. Baldragon allowed him to run, seemingly content to leave the younger brother alive after such a humiliating defeat.

However, when Edward finally returned to Dundee, he found that the dragon had beaten him there. Baldragon had unleashed his fury upon the town, burning a vast stretch of farmland and houses and leaving death and destruction in his wake. The damage was so severe that even those who had survived the attack were at risk of starvation during the coming winter. With Martin dead, his parents long gone, and his home destroyed, Edward found himself entirely alone. With little left to lose, he took the remains of his damaged boat and sailed south towards Edinburgh in search of help.

Apprenticeship under Organic Jim

Upon arriving in Edinburgh, Edward encountered Organic Jim, a wise and powerful master of the magical arts. After hearing Edward's story, Jim took pity on the young man and agreed to accept him as an apprentice. For the first time in his life, Edward had found someone capable of offering him a way forward. Rather than allowing his grief and hatred to consume him, Jim taught him how to turn his anger into something useful, beginning a long period of training that would fundamentally change Edward.

Jim's lessons were not limited to magic. He trained Edward to improve his physical strength and speed, teaching him to combine his natural abilities with magical techniques. Edward also learned to channel his power through his harmonica, turning what might otherwise have been an ordinary instrument into an important part of his magical practice. Under Jim's guidance, Edward gradually developed into a formidable fighter, although his progress was far from effortless.

Edward was not Jim's only apprentice. Tim, another student of the magical arts, quickly became Edward's rival. The two frequently competed, each seeking to prove themselves the more capable student. Their rivalry pushed Edward to improve, although it also provided a constant reminder that there were others who could challenge him. It would take five years of training before Edward was finally able to defeat Tim, marking a significant milestone in his development.

Following Edward's victory, Jim declared that he was ready for his final lesson. Having taught him everything he considered suitable for an ordinary apprentice, Jim revealed the existence of the Forbidden Arts. These powerful and dangerous techniques offered strength far beyond what conventional magic could provide, but came at an extraordinary cost to the user. The Forbidden Arts did not simply consume magical energy; they threatened the very mind of the person wielding them. Despite the danger, Edward accepted the knowledge, believing it to be the final weapon he needed to defeat Baldragon.

Battle against Baldragon

In 1285, twenty years after Baldragon first attacked Dundee, Edward finally returned to confront the dragon. No longer the frightened young man who had fled the town six years earlier, Edward had become a powerful magician and fighter under Organic Jim's tutelage. He returned with a clear purpose: to avenge his family, free Dundee, and ensure that Baldragon would never again threaten the lives of its people.

Upon reaching Baldragon, Edward demanded that the dragon face him. Baldragon, recognising the young man as the survivor of Martin's failed rebellion, was delighted by the opportunity to torment him once again. He mocked Edward's training, insulted Organic Jim, and made cruel remarks about Martin and the rest of his family. The dragon seemed to take particular pleasure in reminding Edward of his previous defeat, believing that no amount of training could make a human capable of challenging him.

The battle quickly became a destructive contest of strength and magic. Baldragon unleashed his flames upon Edward, but found that his opponent was now far too quick and skilled to be easily burned. Edward fought back with the abilities he had spent years developing, using his harmonica to channel his magic and evade the dragon's attacks. For perhaps the first time in his life, Baldragon was faced with an opponent who could genuinely threaten him.

Despite Edward's improvements, however, the dragon remained a formidable opponent. As the battle continued, Baldragon's taunts and mockery became increasingly personal, until Edward's anger overwhelmed his judgement. In desperation, and unwilling to accept another defeat, Edward turned to the Forbidden Arts. He sacrificed his own mind and sanity in exchange for an immense increase in power, unleashing magic that he could no longer fully control.

The newly empowered Edward tore Baldragon from the sky and brought him crashing down upon the ruins of the very houses the dragon had burned years earlier. Baldragon's confidence quickly turned to desperation as he found himself unable to contend with Edward's newfound strength. Realising that his physical form was no longer sufficient to protect him, the dragon attempted one final gambit, sacrificing his size and pouring his remaining strength into his flames in an effort to overwhelm Edward.

This desperate attempt proved insufficient. Edward's magic overwhelmed Baldragon, extinguishing the power that sustained him and draining the dragon of his remaining strength and lifespan. As the battle came to its conclusion, Baldragon's once-mighty body was reduced to a frozen copper husk, curled up in the centre of Dundee. The dragon that had terrorised the town for decades was finally defeated, although the victory came at a terrible cost.

Present day

Edward survived the battle, but the use of the Forbidden Arts had taken an irreversible toll upon his mind. The man who had once sought only to avenge his family had become a maddened and unpredictable figure, his sanity sacrificed alongside the power he had gained. While Baldragon lay defeated, Edward had lost much of the person he had once been.

Having drained Baldragon of his lifespan, Edward found himself able to continue living long beyond the natural limits of a human life. Rather than leaving Dundee to begin a new existence, he chose to remain among the ruins, watching over the town that had taken so much from him. He vowed to stand ready should Baldragon ever return, no matter how long that might take. Even if a thousand years were to pass, Edward would be there to face the dragon once more.

Edward remains in Dundee to this day, serving as its maddened protector. His mind may be broken, and his understanding of the world may have long since deteriorated, but his purpose remains unchanged. Baldragon must never rise again, and Edward intends to ensure that he never does.

Appearance

In the events surrounding the Battle of Dundee Edward looked like a young man in his 20s, and went from wearing fishermens clothing to the robes and loose layers favoured by Organic Jim. He had a pale face that was being perpetually burnt, black hair and eys the color of grass. In the present day, Edward now appears as a man a of average height in his early 60s. He frequently wears a pair of battered trainers, a black scuffed leather jacket, an old t-shirt, and a well loved baseball cap.
