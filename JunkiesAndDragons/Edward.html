Edward

Baldragon the Wicked, Scourge of Dundee, Tyrant of Dundee Law, foe of Edward.

Overview

"You took them from me! My home, my parents, my sister-in-law and my brother. Mother, father, Fincana and Martin, friends and family. Curse you! Curse you, you wretched thing! I will kill you today you flying snake, I shall tear your soul apart! I am Edward, and mine is the name you shall forever fear!" - Edward

Edward was born in the town of Dundee in the summer of 1259. His family were fishers and trawled along the river Tay, leaving his upbringing mainly in the hands of his brother Martin. In the year 1265 Baldragon arrived in Dundee amid fire and flames. Martin managed to keep Edward safe and their parents were out fishing at the time of the attack, although their house was lost in the attack. 3 years later in 1268, another attack killed the boys parents, leaving Martin and Edward struggling to continue the family business until the sacrifices starts.

In 1279, a few days before Edwards 20th birthday, his sister-in-law Fincana was chosen to be the nineth sacrifice to Baldragon. Martin was enraged by the villages willingness to sacrifice his fiance and dragged Edward in a mad quest to try and kill Baldragon. The quest failed and resulted in Martins death in the flames of Baldragon. Edward survived only by running back to Dundee, but when he arrived he found ruins and death. Baldragon had beaten him there and made his fury known, burning a vast swathe of farmland and houses. If so many had not died in the attack it is doubtful that they would have survived the starvation and winter. Fearful and alone, Edward took his damaged boat and sailed to the city of Edinburgh to try and get help.

Upon arriving in Edinburgh Edward encountered Organic Jim, a wise master of the magical arts. Jim took Edward on as an apprentice after hearing his tale, teaching Edward how to use his strength, speed, and magical abilities. Tim, Jim's other apprentice, became a rival to Edward, and it would take five years of training before Edward could best him. Upon Edwards victory, Jim declared Edward ready for his final lesson, and revelaed the Forbidden Arts to him. Edward then left to return to Dundee and face Baldragon once and for all.

In the year 1285, 20 years after Baldragon first attacked Dundee, Edward returned to face down Baldragon once and for all. Baldragon mocked Edward, insulting his training, his master, and his dead brother. Cackling and mocking, Baldragon unwittingly caused his own demise by angering Edward into using the Forbidden Arts. Sacrificing his own mind and sanity for more power, Edward tore Baldragon from the sky and smote the drake upon the ruins of houses he had once burned. Unable to contend with an enraged and empowered Edward, Baldragon found himself overwhlemed, and in turn sacrificed his size for more power. This desperate attempt was not enough to do more than cling to life as his body succumbed to Edwards magics, resulting in Edwards draining Baldragon of his power and lifespan until Baldragon was a frozen copper husk curled in the center of Dundee town. Edward, his mind succumbing to the insanity of the Forbidden Arts, vowed to stand ready for when Baldragon would return, even if it would take a thousand years.

Edward remains in Dundee to this day, his stolen lifespan allowing him to remain Dundee's maddened protector.

History

Origins

It is not known when exactly Baldragon was born, although, judging by his size and the lack of other stories including him by the time of his intombment, it was likely sometime around the year 900. Like all dragons, Baldragon was born in the center of an active volcano in the Fiery North. Not much is known of his youth or family line, although it is unlikely he is part of any royal bloodline. It is rumoured that the reason he left is because of a dispute with a sibling, the fact that Baldragon left indicating that he lost that particular fight.

Coming to Dundee

Baldragon flew down from the Fiery North suprisingly stealthy for a dragon, avoiding large cities to focus on smaller less defended towns and villages. While the exact amount of time Baldragon spent roaming the wilderness is not known, it is a fact that in 1265 he came across the town of Dundee. He descended upon the town like a wrathful tornado wreathed in flames, snatching up townsfolk and livestock alike. He proclaimed that he was a merciful dragon even as he feasted upon his catch, and with fangs dripping blood stated that he would settle for one townsfolk a year in exchange for not burning down the rest of the town. The terrified citizens agreed, fighting the fires and collecting the dead. True to his word, Baldragon did not attack again, slumbering atop Dundee Law most of the time except for when a new sacrifice was sent.

The death of Martin

On the 9th year of Baldragon's reign, he ate Martin's fiance. Martin, first orphaned by Baldragon's initial attack, now bereaved by the same cruel fires and fangs, declared that he would no longer tolerate the dragon tyrant. Martin and his younger brother Edward trained for a year before trying to slay Baldragon on the day of the next sacrifice. As Martin swung his sword Baldragon laughed, cackling as the blade shattered against his scales. His cackles grew hotter and hotter until Martin was cooked alive, only granted the release of death when the dragons jaws snapped his head off. Baldragon let Edward run, mocking him and cursing him to an eventual madness, before settling down to digest his meal.

Battle against Edward

In the year 1285 Edward returned to face down Baldragon once and for all. Having trained under Organic Jim, Edward had learnt how to channel his power threw his harmonica. Upon reaching Dundee Law he cried out, demanding vengeance against the dragon. Baldragon mocked Edward, insulting his training, his master, and his dead brother. Cackling and cruel, Baldragon tried to burn Edward, but his flames were too slow. Delighted by this challange Baldragon battled Edward, taunting and tormenting his foe even as they wrought destruction on the land. The dragon unwittingly caused his own demise by angering Edward, who, when his power seemed insufficient, fell into the use of the Forbidden Hexes no matter the cost to his mind and soul. Unable to contend with this dark, maddened Edward, Baldragon found himself overwhlemed. The drake sacrificed his size for more power, pouring his physical body into his flames in an attempt to destroy Edward. This desperate attempt failed, and with Baldragon so weakened, Edward extinguished the fires in his heart and petrified Baldragon into a statue.

Present day

Baldragon lies frozen forever in the center of Dundee. What is left of his soul writhes in agony, trying futilely to reignite his flame and break free. His efforts are in vain, for as long as Edward still lives, maddened or not, Baldragon will never be able to break free of his power.

Appearance

In the events surrounding the Battle of Dundee Edward looked like a young man in his 20s, and went from wearing fishermens clothing to the robes and loose layers favoured by Organic Jim. He had a pale face that was being perpetually burnt, black hair and eys the color of grass. In the present day, Edward now appears as a man a of average height in his early 60s. He frequently wears a pair of battered trainers, a black scuffed leather jacket, an old t-shirt, and a well loved baseball cap.
