Baldragon

Baldragon the Wicked, Scourge of Dundee, Tyrant of Dundee Law, foe of Edward.

Overview

"Baldragon, The Wicked, the Tyrant of Dundee Law, Great Devourer of Men, Great Dragon of the North-East, and soon to be murderer of my love. Nigh on ten years has this wretched creature menaced the world, slaughtering man and woman and child. I say NO MORE!" - Martin

Born in the infernal pits in the center of a volcano in the Fiery North, Baldragon the Dragon emerged into the world of men in 1265, soaring through the world before settling by the town of Dundee at the Dundee Law. Once a year he would claim a citizen and feast upon them. When Martin tried to fight back, Baldragon slew him with flaming laughter. The echoes of the laughter followed Edward, cursing him to his eventual madness.

In the year 1285, 20 years after Baldragon first attacked Dundee, Edward returned to face down Baldragon once and for all. Baldragon mocked Edward, insulting his training, his master, and his dead brother. Cackling and mocking, Baldragon unwittingly caused his own demise by angering Edward into using the Forbidden Arts. Unable to contend with an enraged and empowered Edward, Baldragon found himself overwhlemed, sacrificing his size for more power. Even this desperate attempt was not enough to do more than cling to life as his body succumbed to Edwards magics.

Baldragon now lies petrified, frozen forever as a statue in Dundee town main street. Even as he writhes and struggles, he has not the strength or power to escape his prison while Edward remains nearby.

History

Origins

It is not known when exactly Baldragon was born, although, judging by his size and the lack of other stories including him by the time of his intombment, it was likely sometime around the year 900. Like all dragons, Baldragon was born in the center of an active volcano in the Fiery North. Not much is known of his youth or family line, although it is unlikely he is part of any royal bloodline. It is rumoured that the reason he left is because of a dispute with a sibling, the fact that Baldragon left indicating that he lost that particular fight.

Coming to Dundee

Baldragon flew down from the Fiery North suprisingly stealthy for a dragon, avoiding large cities to focus on smaller less defended towns and villages. While the exact amount of time Baldragon spent roaming the wilderness is not known, it is a fact that in 1265 he came across the town of Dundee. He descended upon the town like a wrathful tornado wreathed in flames, snatching up townsfolk and livestock alike. He proclaimed that he was a merciful dragon even as he feasted upon his catch, and with fangs dripping blood stated that he would settle for one townsfolk a year in exchange for not burning down the rest of the town. The terrified citizens agreed, fighting the fires and collecting the dead. True to his word, Baldragon did not attack again, slumbering atop Dundee Law most of the time except for when a new sacrifice was sent.

The death of Martin

On the 9th year of Baldragon's reign, he ate Martin's fiance. Martin, first orphaned by Baldragon's initial attack, now bereaved by the same cruel fires and fangs, declared that he would no longer tolerate the dragon tyrant. Martin and his younger brother Edward trained for a year before trying to slay Baldragon on the day of the next sacrifice. As Martin swung his sword Baldragon laughed, cackling as the blade shattered against his scales. His cackles grew hotter and hotter until Martin was cooked alive, only granted the release of death when the dragons jaws snapped his head off. Baldragon let Edward run, mocking him and cursing him to an eventual madness, before settling down to digest his meal.

Battle against Edward

In the year 1285 Edward returned to face down Baldragon once and for all. Having trained under Organic Jim, Edward had learnt how to channel his power threw his harmonica. Upon reaching Dundee Law he cried out, demanding vengeance against the dragon. Baldragon mocked Edward, insulting his training, his master, and his dead brother. Cackling and cruel, Baldragon tried to burn Edward, but his flames were too slow. Delighted by this challange Baldragon battled Edward, taunting and tormenting his foe even as they wrought destruction on the land. The dragon unwittingly caused his own demise by angering Edward, who, when his power seemed insufficient, fell into the use of the Forbidden Hexes no matter the cost to his mind and soul. Unable to contend with this dark madness Edward, Baldragon found himself overwhlemed. The drake sacrificed his size for more power, pouring his physical body into his flames in an attempt to destroy Edward. This desperate attempt failed, and with Baldragon so weakened, Edward extinguished the fires in his heart and petrified Baldragon into a statue.

Present day

Baldragon lies frozen forever in the center of Dundee. What is left of his soul writhes in agony, trying futilely to reignite his flame and break free. His efforts are in vain, for as long as Edward still lives, maddened or not, Baldragon will never be able to break free of his power.

Appearance

Baldragon was on the smaller side for a dragon, at roughly 5 meters snout to tailtip. His scales in life were a reflective blue-green, the same hue as the aged copper that now traps him. His wingspan was well over 20 meters in width and shimmered in the light, something he used as an intimidation tactic. His eyes burned bright blue, glowing with the light of his fire at all times.
