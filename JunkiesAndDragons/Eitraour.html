Eitraður

Eitraður the Wicked, Scourge of Dundee, Tyrant of Dundee Law, foe of Edward.

Overview

"Eitraður, The Wicked, the Tyrant of Dundee Law, Great Devourer of Men, Great Dragon of the North-East, and the murderer of my love. 500 years has this wretched creature menaced the world, slaughtering man and woman and child. I say NO MORE!" - Martin

Born in the fiery pits in the center of a volcano in the Fiery North, Eitraður the Dragon emerged into the world of men in 2015, soaring through the world before settling by the town of Dundee at the Dundee Law. Once a year he would claim a citizen and feast upon them. When Martin tried to fight back, Eitraður slew him with flaming laughter. The echoes of the laughter followed Edward, cursing him to his eventual madness.

In the year 2026 Edward returned to face down Eitraður once and for all. Eitraður mocked Edward, insulting his training, his master, and his dead brother. Cackling and mocking, Eitraður unwittingly caused his own demise by angering Edward into using the Forbidden Arts. Unable to contend with an enraged and empowered Edward, Eitraður found himself overwhlemed, sacrificing his size for more power. Even this desperate attempt was not enough to do more than cling to life as his body succumbed to Edwards magics.

Eitraður now lies petrified, frozen forever as a statue in Dundee town main street. Even as he writhes and struggles, he has not the strength or power to escape his prison while Edward remains nearby.

History

Origins

Like all dragons, Eitraður was born in the center of an active volcano in the Fiery North. Not much is known of his youth or family line, although it is unlikely he is part of any royal bloodline. It is rumoured that the reason he left is because of a dispute with a sibling, the fact that Eitraður left indicating that he lost that particular fight.

Coming to Dundee

Eitraður flew down from the Fiery North suprisingly stealthy for a dragon, avoiding large cities to focus on smaller less defended towns and villages. While the exact amount of time Eitraður spent roaming the wilderness is not known, it is a fact that in 2015 he came across the town of Dundee. He descended upon the town like a wrathful tornado wreathed in flames, snatching up townsfolk and livestock alike. He proclaimed that he was a merciful dragon even as he feasted upon his catch, and with fangs dripping blood stated that he would settle for one townsfolk a year in exchange for not burning down the rest of the town. The terrified citizens agreed, fighting the fires and collecting the dead. True to his word, Eitraður did not attack again, slumbering atop Dundee Law most of the time except for when a new sacrifice was sent.

The death of Martin

On the 9th year of Eitraður's reign, he ate Martin's fiance. Martin, first orphaned by Eitraður's initial attack, now bereaved by the same cruel fires and fangs, declared that he would no longer tolerate the dragon tyrant. Martin and his younger brother Edward trained for a year before trying to slay Eitraður on the day of the next sacrifice. As Martin swung his sword Eitraður laughed, cackling as the blade shattered against his scales. His cackles grew hotter and hotter until Martin was cooked alive, only granted the release of death when the dragons jaws snapped his head off. Eitraður let Edward run, mocking him and cursing him to an eventual madness, before settling down to digest his meal.

Battle against Edward

In the year 2026 Edward returned to face down Eitraður once and for all. Having trained under Organic Jim, Edward had learnt how to channel his power threw his harmonica. Upon reaching Dundee Law he cried out, demanding vengeance against the dragon. Eitraður mocked Edward, insulting his training, his master, and his dead brother. Cackling and cruel, Eitraður tried to burn Edward, but his flames were too slow. Delighted by this challange Eitraður battled Edward, taunting and tormenting his foe even as they wrought destruction on the land. The dragon unwittingly caused his own demise by angering Edward, who, when his power seemed insufficient, fell into the use of the Forbidden Hexes no matter the cost to his mind and soul. Unable to contend with this dark madness Edward, Eitraður found himself overwhlemed. The drake sacrificed his size for more power, pouring his physical body into his flames in an attempt to destroy Edward. This desperate attempt failed, and with Eitraður so weakened, Edward extinguished the fires in his heart and petrified Eitraður into a statue.

Present day

Eitraður lies frozen forever in the center of Dundee. What is left of his soul writhes in agony, trying futilely to reignite his flame and break free. His efforts are in vain, for as long as Edward still lives, maddened or not, Eitraður will never be able to break free of his power.
