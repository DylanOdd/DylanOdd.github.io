History

Origins

In the beginning the universe was made by the Twin Gods. Each took responsibility for half of Their creation. One demanded total obedience and order, while the Other allowed life and civilisation to grow wild. It is in this God's domain that we find ourselves in... Once upon a time, in a far off corner of a young galaxy, there is a planet. It is blue and green, and orbits its star at the perfect distance for life to flourish. And flourish it has. The most prominant intelligent species of this planet is a type of crustacean that inhabits the lower levels of the ocean, and has created the closest thing to a utopia in that galaxy. The second most prominant species is a type of mostly hairless ape with large brains and larger egos. These apes call themselves humans, and despite their overinflated senses of things like self-inportance, vengeance, and fashion, are overall considered a net positive to the world as a whole. But humans are not alone on this world. In the charred depths of molten mounatins, where volcanos spew rocks and precious minerals, there lie the dragons. Proud and terrible, these creatures consider themselves superior, and have been the enemy of humans for centuries. Many wars have been fought over past millennia, with countless dead on both sides. The dragon population dwindles, unable to sustain itself amid such a cruel and terrible conflict. It is rare these days for a dragon to leave their volcanic realm. And yet, in the year 2015 by the human calender, one did...
